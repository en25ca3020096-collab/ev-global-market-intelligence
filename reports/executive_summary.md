# Executive Business Intelligence Report: Global EV Industry

**Prepared For:**
* **Emily Johnson**, EV Market Strategy Director
* **David Chen**, Automotive Business Intelligence Manager

**Reporting Period:** 2021 – 2025  
**Data Scope:** 1,250 Model Configurations across 18 Global Manufacturers and 6 Continents  

---

## 1. Executive Summary: Emily Johnson (Market Strategy)

### Key Strategic Findings:
1. **SUV Dominance with Two-Wheeler Volume Growth:**
   * **Electric SUVs** represent the largest single revenue and volume driver, capturing **37.83%** of total units sold (54.19M units) with an average selling price of **$55,265**.
   * **Sedans** form the premium core, generating **25.23%** of unit sales with the highest average driving range (**709 km**) and exceptional customer satisfaction (**4.87 / 5.0**).
   * **Two-Wheelers** represent the fastest micro-mobility growth segment, contributing **20.58%** of global unit sales at an accessible average price point of **$6,760**.

2. **Customer Satisfaction Quadrant Analysis:**
   * Vehicle models offering **>500 km range** with battery fast-charging under 30 minutes consistently score in the top tier (>4.7/5.0).
   * Hatchbacks exhibit high price sensitivity; models priced below \$25,000 using LFP batteries maintain strong ratings (4.55/5.0) when combined with reliable urban range (350–450 km).

### Strategic Recommendations:
* **Product Portfolio Mix:** Expand mid-tier SUV offerings (\$35,000–\$48,000 range) to capture the expanding family vehicle transition.
* **Regional Electrification:** Accelerate commercial two-wheeler and hatchback expansion in high-density urban markets across Asia and Southern Europe.

## 2. Competitive Intelligence: David Chen (OEM & Production Benchmarks)

### Manufacturer Market Share & Production Insights:
1. **The Global OEM Duopoly:**
   * **Tesla** leads global sales volume with a **17.87%** market share (25.6M units), powered primarily by its NCA and NMC vehicle architectures in North America and China.
   * **BYD** closely follows with **15.27%** market share (21.88M units), leveraging vertical integration and blade LFP chemistry dominance in China and expanding export markets.
   * Traditional legacy OEMs (**BMW at 6.76%**, **Volkswagen at 5.65%**, **Hyundai at 4.35%**) are narrowing the gap through dedicated 800V EV modular platforms.
   * Regional champions like **Tata Motors (6.82%)** and **Ather Energy (6.34%)** demonstrate that targeted domestic battery manufacturing creates defensible market moats.

2. **Geographical Manufacturing Hubs:**
   * **China** remains the primary assembly and cell supply chain hub, commanding over 45% of total manufacturing capacity.
   * **United States** and **Germany** lead high-margin premium sedan and electric pickup production.
   * **India** is emerging as a global scale hub for cost-effective LFP two-wheelers and compact SUVs.

### Consulting Recommendations:
* **Supply Chain Diversification:** Advise new automotive entrants to secure dual-source battery supply agreements (LFP for entry models; NMC/Solid-State for premium flagships).
* **Cost-per-Range Parity:** Maintain vehicle price-to-range ratios below **$85/km** to remain competitive against aggressive price reductions from market leaders.
